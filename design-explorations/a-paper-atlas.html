Fonts
Fraunces (display, soft optical serif, wonky italic for the one accented phrase) · IBM Plex Sans (text) · IBM Plex Mono (paths, numbers, labels). All self-hosted.
Buttons
Chunky: vermilion block, 2px ink border, 4px hard ink shadow that collapses when pressed. Secondary is a mono uppercase arrow button on the table.
Cards
Paper sheets with grain, a deckle shadow on the table and varied rotation (−1.2° to 0.8°). Tape, fold lines, rubber stamps and pencil notes appear only where they say something.
Signature
The compass needle in the logo tracks your pointer, then spins and settles north while a repo is being analysed. On Blast Radius, rings spread across the map hop by hop.
Why it fits
The product is literally an atlas of a codebase. A dark table makes the paper (the evidence) the brightest thing on screen; one hot red ink means “this will break”.
